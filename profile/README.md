# Center for Cooperative Media

**An initiative of the College of Communication and Media at Montclair State University**

Our mission is to grow and strengthen local and collaborative journalism in New Jersey and beyond.

---

## About Us

The Center for Cooperative Media supports collaborative journalism and strengthens local news ecosystems. We work with journalists, news organizations, and communities to foster innovation in journalism and build sustainable models for local news.

### What We Do

- **Collaborative Reporting** — We coordinate multi-newsroom journalism projects that serve communities across New Jersey
- **Training & Resources** — We provide tools, training, and support to help local news organizations thrive
- **Research & Innovation** — We study what works in local news and develop new models for sustainability
- **Community Building** — We convene journalists and news leaders to share knowledge and build partnerships
